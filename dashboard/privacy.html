Ownvista HQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Ownvista HQ is a private, internal tool operated by Ownvista for its owner's own business use. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner signs in to it.

Google account data

Ownvista HQ requests access to the owner's own Gmail account (scope gmail.modify) only to create draft emails and read replies to messages the tool itself prepared. It does not access any other person's Google account.

Ownvista HQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store

Draft text, message metadata needed to track replies, and operating logs are stored in the owner's private database. OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No human other than the account owner reads Gmail data, except where required by law.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke Ownvista HQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Ownvista HQ's Google sign-in screen.
